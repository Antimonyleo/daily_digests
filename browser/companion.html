Open DailyDigest

Your digest, ready each morning

The optional Companion is a program you run on your own computer. It lets the website brew while its tab is closed, email a digest, or use your configured summary provider. Your computer must stay on and awake.

You do not need it to read, brew, rate, or save in the website. The local DailyDigest app already includes these capabilities and needs no Companion.

Choose the benefit you need

  • Morning brewing: prepare a digest from a copy of your reading desk at its configured hour (8 am by default), in its saved timezone.
  • Email: configure delivery separately. Reading reply ratings also requires separate mailbox settings.
  • Optional AI summaries: use a configured provider or installed Claude Code / Codex command, with credentials kept on your computer. Provider usage limits and charges still apply.

Set up automatic brewing

  1. Install DailyDigest on the computer that will do the work.
  2. In the website, open More → Data & compute → Companion. Select “Prepare a command for automatic morning brewing,” copy it, and run it from the DailyDigest folder.
  3. Keep the terminal open. Copy its address and the token from the file it names into the website, then select Connect and test.
  4. Select Send reading desk to Companion. This explicitly transfers your topics, ratings, history, and schedule settings. No brew can be scheduled until you send a copy.
  5. Keep the computer awake and connected at the scheduled hour. Get Companion’s latest copy when you want to read the result here.

These are separate copies, not automatic synchronization. Getting the Companion’s copy replaces the desk in this browser. New browser ratings since your last send are not merged. Download a backup before switching copies. Email is not enabled by scheduling.

What access does it have?

It listens on localhost and checks an allowed website origin and a private token. Keep the token private. A browser may ask for local-network permission; grant it only for the site and helper you intend to connect.

For online summaries, selected article details and a short profile go to your chosen provider. Provider credentials remain on the Companion computer unless you explicitly include credentials in a state transfer. Mailbox access is only needed if you configure importing reply ratings.

Stop or disconnect

Press Ctrl+C in the Companion terminal to stop the helper and its scheduling. Disconnect in the website removes its connection there; it does not stop the running helper. To revoke a copied token, stop the helper, remove its token file, restart, and reconnect with the new token. Keep a backup before removing stored reading desks.
